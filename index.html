Practical

The handful of things that are annoying to discover late.

Ship the festival gear. Takkyubin it from Minakami on the 12th to the Tokyo hotel for the 20th, or to Eri's. A tent has no business on the Jomon Sugi trail, and you are on planes and ferries for four days straight after the festival.
Skip the rail pass. Roughly ¥47,000 of shinkansen between the 16th and the 20th, against ¥50,000 for a 7-day national pass — it does not pay off, and that pass excludes Nozomi and Mizuho anyway. Regional Kyushu / Sanyo passes are worth a second look if you want to be sure.
Vegetarian, in practice. Dashi — bonito stock — is in most broths, miso soup, simmered vegetables and many dipping sauces, so a lot of plant-looking food is not. Tokyo has real range and Jonas now has five nights there; Eri's kitchen covers the 16th. Yakushima and the Kyushu leg are the thin part, and the Jomon Sugi day needs food carried in.
Cash on Yakushima. The rest of the trip takes cards fine; the island is the exception. 7-Eleven ATMs take foreign cards, and mobile Suica in Apple Wallet handles transit.
Two Tokyo nights, so split them. Stay wherever you like on the 19th, then move out to Kamata or a Haneda hotel for the 20th. A 04:30 start from central Tokyo is a taxi; from the airport side it is ten minutes.

Fixed anchors: Labyrinth Oct 10–12 (Minakami Hodaigi), the Haneda–Kagoshima–Yakushima flights on Oct 12, and Jonas' 07:50 departure on Oct 21.
Where it splits: Oct 17. Kumamoto to Okayama is one direct train, ~2 h 20. Hiroshima and Itsukushima sit on that same line as an optional detour — Jonas is skipping it, everyone else decides for themselves. Same bed in Okayama either way.
Where it splits again: Oct 19. Jonas goes Okayama → Tokyo, ~3 h 15, and skips Kansai entirely; the rest go Okayama → Kyoto, ~1 h. This removes the old problem where the last night ran Kyoto → Tokyo on an evening train against a 04:30 alarm — there is now no train at all on the 20th.
Watch: Yakushima weather (typhoon tail) can cancel the Oct 15 ferry — Oct 14 is the buffer to burn. Naoshima museums are shut on Mondays and want advance tickets.
