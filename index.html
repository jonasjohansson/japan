Fixed anchors: Labyrinth Oct 10–12 (Minakami Hodaigi), the Haneda–Kagoshima–Yakushima flights on Oct 12, and Jonas' 07:50 departure on Oct 21.
Where it splits: Oct 17. Kumamoto to Okayama is one direct train, ~2 h 20. Hiroshima and Itsukushima sit on that same line as an optional detour — Jonas is skipping it, everyone else decides for themselves. Same bed in Okayama either way.
The 20th: Jonas cannot leave Kyoto on the morning of the 21st — the first Nozomi out is ~06:15 and reaches Tokyo ~08:25, half an hour after the plane. An evening train on the 20th is the only safe version, and not the last one: be on something by 18:00 so there are still trains behind you if one is cancelled.
Watch: Yakushima weather (typhoon tail) can cancel the Oct 15 ferry — Oct 14 is the buffer to burn. Naoshima museums are shut on Mondays and want advance tickets.
